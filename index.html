Field Notes

Why passwords fail in practice

Attackers do not guess randomly. They start with leaked habits: seasons, names, years, keyboard paths, reused fragments, and easy substitutions.

Weak Summer2024!

Season plus year plus symbol is one of the first patterns tested.

Stronger harbor-lime-velvet-47-lantern

Longer, unusual, memorable, and far more resistant to common attacks.

Strong Password Tips

How to make passwords harder to crack

Use more words

Four or more unrelated words are easier to remember and harder to brute force.

Avoid personal clues

Do not use names, birthdays, school names, sports teams, or current years.

Keep each password unique

Use a different password for every account so one leak cannot unlock everything.

Store it safely

Use a password manager and turn on MFA for email, banking, and important accounts.
